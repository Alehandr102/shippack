Diana Henderson is dead and the ring is mine. To the last she would not believe her own sister had come to this, and she was wrong by exactly half: both of them had. A fine family, and the part left to me in it suits the rest of them...
#TEXT	4
Viki took the ring without a word, turned it over in her fingers and put it out of sight. Not another word about the sister - she asked for that herself. One aboard, three to go.
#TEXT	5
Mishel Lus was taken down into the Tortuga catacombs by two men in long robes. Skeletons met me below, and it was no draught that put them on their feet: somebody disturbed the old vaults very recently. Now work out who I am hunting down here - kidnappers or the dead...
#TEXT	6
By the way out to Fish Eye Bay I found a woman's medallion on a torn chain. No body anywhere near, which means Mishel was taken away alive, and taken by sea. Rebekka should see it - a sister will know her own medallion.
#TEXT	7
The medallion is hers - Rebekka knew it at a glance. She promises to find out within three days whose ship left Fish Eye that night, and asked me to wait in the room above the tavern. Well then, three days is no great wait on Tortuga.
#TEXT	8
Rebekka found out nothing about the ship, but she did catch one of the abductors in her own house. Upstairs there were two dead captains, one living satanist and a blow that kept me off my feet for three days. And the satanist's body was gone from the house, though he was killed twice over! Only the medallion is left, and I am taking it to Kendart.
#TEXT	9
The medallion belongs to a sect that calls itself the animatists. Fifteen years ago Beliz lost its governor because of them, and the fort garrison lost three quarters of its men. The hundred thousand offered for the investigation went to one Meril Kreno, who wrote a paper about swamp fever and lives in Beliz to this day, in the house beside the port office. He is where I start.
#TEXT	10
Kreno will talk only aboard his own ship, and not a word before that. She lies in Chetumal Bay, and the road there runs through the jungle. His caution does not sit well with me: a man calls you onto his own deck when he wants to pick the ground himself...
#TEXT	11
Four days in the hold of the brig Cerebus ended today! They took my weapons back in the bay, barred the door from outside, and Kreno came down twice to boast of the ransom he would be paid for me. The lock gave to a woman's hairpin out of a crate, and my own gear was lying right there in the hold - he could not be bothered to throw it over the side. A sabre in the wardroom, and guards further along the decks.
#TEXT	15
Casander is dead and the brig is left without a master. And my own ship came in to the same shore: the crew went looking for their captain and found him. They call this stretch Muhachi's shore, and somewhere here is where the men in black robes are to be looked for.
#TEXT	16
The satanists are cut down, Meril Kreno fell in the same fight, and Mishel Lus is free. Muhachi explained the part I had not understood: their medallions give protection but take a life for it, and that is why they always need new people. Now to Beliz, to find a berth for the captured brig and the men aboard her, and from there to Tortuga.
#TEXT	17
In Beliz I was met by Terry Lorto, a gunner Casander had kept at the oars. The captured brig is settled, and Lorto, I dare say, will yet make a name for himself. All that is left is to take Mishel to her sister on Tortuga.
#TEXT	18
The Lus sisters are aboard. Rebekka only said she did not know what she could pay me with, and I did not explain that this work is paid for already. Let her think she was lucky in her captain.
#TEXT	19
Natali Remis was kept by a pirate called Ortodokse Malkin - kept until today. Three of his men are still sitting aboard his brig, and while they are there Natali will not leave the Bermudas. So the brig it is.
#TEXT	20
Malkin's brig is clear and Natali is aboard. I have no more business on the Bermudas.
#TEXT	21
All four are with me. Off to San Juan, to hand Kendart his restless cargo!
#TEXT	22
The house in San Juan is open, the corsairs are content and Kendart is twice as content. For a reward he handed me a batch of forty-two pounder guns: they can be fitted once, and only to a ship of second class or better. The shipwright at the yard has been told - now I need a hull to match!
#TEXT	23
Rebekka Lus will go to San Juan only together with her sister. Three nights ago Mishel was taken into the town catacombs of Tortuga by two men in long robes, and nobody has seen her since. The persuading can wait - the catacombs come first.
